# Code of Conduct

## Expected Conduct

Treat contributors with respect.

Discuss technical evidence, behavior, and outcomes.

Give clear criticism without personal attacks.

Protect private data and security reports.

Accept correction when evidence disproves a claim.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against another person.

Do not publish private information without permission.

Do not disrupt project work or security response.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems through a private maintainer channel.

Maintainers can edit, hide, or remove harmful content.

Maintainers can restrict participation after a documented review.
